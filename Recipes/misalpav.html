Misal Pav

Description

Misal Pav is a famous Maharashtrian dish. It is made with cooked sprouts and spicy gravy. It is served with soft bread called pav. It is spicy, tasty, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked sprouts
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 teaspoons misal masala
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups water
  • Farsan
  • Fresh coriander
  • 4 pav (bread rolls)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add onion and tomatoes. Cook until soft.
  4. Add misal masala and mix well.
  5. Add the cooked sprouts, salt, and water.
  6. Cook for 10 - 15 minutes.
  7. Pour the misal into a bowl.
  8. Add farsan and fresh coriander on top.
  9. Serve hot with buttered pav.
