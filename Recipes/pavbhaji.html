Pav Bhaji

Description

Pav Bhaji is a famous Indian street food. It is made with mashed vegetables cooked with spices. It is served with soft bread rolls called pav. It is tasty, spicy, and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 2 Potatoes
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1 capsicum (chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 teaspoons pav bhaji masala
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander
  • 4 pav (bread rolls)

Steps

  1. Boil and mash the potatoes and peas.
  2. Cook the onion, capsicum, and tomatoes in butter.
  3. Add the spices and mix well.
  4. Add the mashed vegetables and cook for 5–10 minutes..
  5. Toast the pav with butter.
  6. Serve the hot bhaji with butter and coriander.
